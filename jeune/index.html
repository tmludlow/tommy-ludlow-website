Questions

How do I know which blend to take?

Use the cycle tracker. Enter the first day of your last period and your usual cycle length, and it shows today's blend. You take the Essential Blend every day, plus one phase blend.

What if my cycle is irregular, or I use hormonal contraception?

The phase schedule is built around a regular natural cycle. If your cycle varies or you use hormonal contraception, the days may not match your body. The Essential Blend can be taken on any schedule. Please check with your healthcare provider before starting a supplement.

Is it safe if I'm pregnant, nursing or taking medication?

Talk to your healthcare provider before taking any supplement, especially if you are pregnant, nursing, have a medical condition or take medication.

What does liposomal mean?

Liposomal delivery wraps nutrients in tiny fat-based spheres called liposomes, with the aim of improving how they are carried and absorbed. How much this helps varies by nutrient.
